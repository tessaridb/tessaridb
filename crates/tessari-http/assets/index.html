    What is not here yet
    
          
    
            What this section shows is what the node itself knows: its own
            membership, the peers it has been told about, and the addresses it
            answers on. That is real, and it is all that is real today.
          
    
          
    
            Replication and failover are still being built.
            There is no leader, no follower, no automatic promotion and no
            quorum. A second node is a second store; nothing copies between them
            yet. The backup and the log replay are the durability story for now,
            and they are the ones to plan around.
